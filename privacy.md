# Privacy Policy — YouTube Uploader

**Last updated: 11 October 2026**

YouTube Uploader is a private tool used by the site owner to upload videos to his own YouTube channel.

## What data it uses

- When you sign in with Google, the app receives an OAuth token that lets it upload videos to your YouTube channel. The token is stored only on the computer running the uploader.
- The app reads video files, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails from folders you choose on that computer, and sends them to Google's YouTube API to perform the upload.

## What it does not do

- It does not collect, store or share your personal data with anyone.
- It does not send your data to any server other than Google's YouTube API, which is required to upload the videos.
- It does not use your data for advertising, analytics or any other purpose.
