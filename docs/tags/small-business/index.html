86% of small businesses use AI. Only 15% actually run on it.

Goldman Sachs' October 2026 report finds 86% of small businesses now use AI, up from 56% two years ago — but only 15% have fully integrated it into their core operations. The gap between trying AI and running on it is now the whole ballgame.
