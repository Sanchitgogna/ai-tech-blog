13 November 2026

Google's new AI can't write a single sentence. That's the point.

Google announced Gemini Decision 1 on 11 November: a model that skips text generation entirely and scores candidate answers in a single pass — up to 1,000 questions per request, 150–250 ms, $0.10 per million input tokens, zero for output. It is the latest in a sudden wave of decision-only models, and it turns the cheapest unit in AI from a word into a choice.

29 October 2026

Google's Genie 4 isn't a video generator. It's a place you can walk into — and rewrite mid-stride

Google DeepMind's Genie 4, announced 27 October, generates explorable simulated worlds in real time — with native audio, live prompt editing, and steerable characters. It's a research preview for trusted testers, not a product yet, and its two-minute attention span is the most honest thing about it.
