14 November 2026

Jensen Huang is gone. The AI boom just lost its centre of gravity.

Nvidia announced on 12 November that co-founder Jensen Huang had resigned as president, CEO and director, effective immediately, after 33 years in charge. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO, the board has its first-ever chair, and the first test comes on Tuesday, when Nvidia reports earnings. The filing gives no reason — the reported background involves an affair, a suspected foreign-intelligence link, and an FBI probe — and the open question now is whether the AI boom's most important company can hold its nerve without its founder.

28 October 2026

Anthropic just filed to go public. Its prospectus is the most honest document in AI.

Anthropic's S-1 filing shows revenue up roughly 14-fold in a year, an operating loss that actually shrank, and future payment commitments of about $245 billion. The headline loss is mostly accounting. The real story is a company asking public investors to fund a race it admits it might never profitably finish.

26 October 2026

OpenAI is buying Anthropic's customers — and the discount is the story

The Information reports OpenAI is winning Anthropic's biggest accounts with discounts of up to 90 percent. The revealing part isn't the size of the cuts — it's that price, not capability, decided the deals.
