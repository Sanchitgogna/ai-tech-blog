Anthropic just handed the merge button to its own AI — and the policy file decides what ships

Anthropic's Claude Ship, launched on 23 November, takes a pull request through review, fixes and an approval decision — with the approval policy written as a file in the repo. The bottleneck it attacks is real, and so is the question of who is accountable when the approver is a model.

OpenAI is cutting Cursor off on Thursday. The real story is who owns your editor now.

OpenAI says it will stop supplying models to Cursor on 12 November, after SpaceX bought the editor for $60 billion. Only about 5% of Cursor's traffic runs on OpenAI models — but the change-of-control clause behind the decision is now the most important line in any AI tool's contract.
