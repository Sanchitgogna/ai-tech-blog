Your AI agent just got a front door and a wallet on the same day. The shops aren't ready.

On Friday 13 November, Sierra shipped Poppy 1.0 — the protocol that lets your AI agent walk into a business and introduce itself — and OpenAI shipped ChatGPT Wallet, which lets its dots agents pay. Shopify switched Poppy on for every store, but Amazon blocks agent purchases, United Airlines is stopping agent bookings, and Visa found AI shoppers fail 42.8% of the time.
