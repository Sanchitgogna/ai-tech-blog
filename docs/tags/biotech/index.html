GPT-6.2 designed proteins that stuck to real drug targets. The 10.9% is the honest part.

OpenAI's GPT-6.2 designed small proteins that bound to five real biological targets in lab tests — 35 hits from 322 candidates. The 10.9% hit rate is modest, and OpenAI is unusually candid about why. The real story is that frontier models are now being graded by wet-lab results, not leaderboards.
